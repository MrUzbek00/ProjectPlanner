# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

TEMPLATE — generated from approved project records — NOT APPROVED

The BRD is the stakeholder-facing business case. It states why the organization is funding the work, what business outcome is expected, and who decides. Financial figures, market claims, headcount and cost savings are confirmed inputs or `TBD (Q-###)`. Never estimate a currency amount, a market size or a saving that no source supplies.
