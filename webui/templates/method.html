The method

The workflow reduces uncertainty progressively. Each stage turns unknowns into confirmed facts, records what is still unknown, and ends in a gate that tools/check_gates.py verifies against the records. A gate cannot pass while an earlier one fails.

Three layers: the Markdown records are the source of truth; the Word, Excel and diagrams are generated for people; the machine handoff JSON is generated for engineering. Nothing is ever edited in an output.
